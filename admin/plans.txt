Components:
	1. 18 Cards: 
		1. 4 2-sided Project cards
		2. 2 1-sided Base cards
		3. 6 1-sided Function cards
		4. 6 1-sided Action cards
	2. Game Mat: 
		Sheet of card with Victory Point track,
		4 Boxes for Project cards, Sequence of Play reminder if it fits
	3. 8 10-sided dice (d10)
	4. x1, x10 and +100 markers for Victory Point track (use cubes)

Sequence of Play:
	The game is played in turns. Each turn, the players will move through the following Phases in order:
		- Operations Phase:
			- The player with the HN Influence marker on “their” side of the Track plays first 
			(if the cube is dead centre, Jedburgh player goes first). 
			- A player may play a Function card, executing one of its two functions, 
			or use an Action card from their Network, or pass. 
			- Normally a player will play 1 card from their hand or use 1 Action card at a time; 
			the only exceptions are when a player plays the Influence HN or Build/Manage Network 
			card function they may play an Action Card at the same time. 
			- When played a Function card or revealed Action card is discarded; 
			a concealed Action card is revealed. 
			- A player may always pass; a player with no cards in their hand and no Action cards in 
			their Network must pass. 
			- This Phase ends when both players pass in succession.
		- Projects Phase:
			- Any Projects that have met or exceeded the Time and Budget minimal values printed on 
			their card are now completed and scored. 
			- See below for process. 
			- Remove completed Project cards and dice from their box on the Game Mat; 
			if more Projects remain to be played, the Country player will now replace Project cards 
			in the empty boxes (the player may choose the new project and which box it goes into).
		- Turn End Phase:
			- If all Projects planned to be played in the game have been completed, 
			end the game and determine Victory Level. 
			- If not:
				- Raise the Time value of all Projects in play by 1, unless the Project’s Budget value 
				is currently 0 (zero); then start a new turn with the Draw Cards Phase.

Project Cards:
	1. During the game, up to 4 Projects at a time will be placed in the 4 boxes labelled ABCD on the Game Mat, 
	representing infrastructure projects currently being worked on.

Base Cards and Networks
	There are 2 Base cards in the game: 
		1 x “Country Team” (red);
		1 x Jedburgh team (blue). 
	Each side of card is coded A B C D for the matching box on the Game Mat which contains a Project card.
	Action cards can be placed along the side of a card for use on Projects corresponding to the lettered box. 
	The total assembly of 1 Base plus any number of Action cards is called a Network. 

When more than 1 Action card is placed against a side of card, 
they are stacked and only the topmost card in the stack can be used to affect 
a Project or be affected by a Degrade Network or Use HN Influence function. 
Reveal and Conceal cards may be used on any cards in the Network. 
Any Action card may be moved in a Build/Manage Network, 
but it must become the topmost card on the side it is moved to. 
When rearranging the entire Network after executing a Maintain Network card, 
all cards may be rearranged as the player pleases but they retain their revealed or concealed status.

Function Cards:
	There are 6 Function cards in the game. 
	Five of them have two functions and the player must choose one of them to execute when playing the card. 
	All Function cards are discarded after play.
	
	They are:
		Influence HN:
			Play alone for “1” value, or with 1 Action card from hand or Network 
			(may be any card if from Network). 
			Move cube total number of boxes towards “your” side. 
			Discard Action card if used.
			Use HN Influence. 
			If the HN Influence cube is on “your” side of the track, use as either 
			an Action card of equal value to HN Influence value on 1 Project, or as a Degrade Network card. 
		Reveal/Conceal:
			Reveal: (flip face up) up to 2 cards in enemy Network. Any cards in Network may be revealed. 
			Conceal: 
				Conceal (flip face down) up to 2 cards in own Network. 
				Any cards in Network may be concealed. 
		Build/Manage Network:
			Add up to 2 Action cards from hand to Network 
			(place concealed, on any side and anywhere in stack) 
			or move 1 Action card to any other side of Base card 
			(Action card becomes topmost card on that side and retains revealed/concealed status). 
			Degrade Network. 
			Discard 1 Action card from enemy Network.
			Card must be both revealed and topmost in its stack.

Action cards:
	There are 6 Action cards in the game: 
		3 x “1” value; 
		2 x “2” value, 
		and 1 x “3” value. 
	An Action card must already be in a player’s network to be played on a Project; 
	they may not be played directly out of hand. 
	The card must also be topmost in its stack.
	When playing an Action card, reveal it if it was concealed; if it was already revealed, discard it. 
	Apply the value of the Action card to the Project in the box matching the letter side of the Base card the 
	Action card is on, by raising or lowering the value shown on the die face or faces on the Project card. 
	The Time value of a Project may be raised or lowered by Action card play. 
	The Budget value may only be increased. 
	A die may not be adjusted below its minimum or above its maximum value (that is, 0 and 9 for d10).
	A player may change either or both die face values if applying a “2” or “3” value Action card, 
	but the total number of changes must be equal to the value of the card.

Completing a Project:
	During the Projects Phase, if both dice on a Project card show values 
	that equal or exceed the minimal values printed on the card, the project is completed. 
	Note the die values, calculate the project’s Completion Cost, and award the Jedburgh player any 
	Victory Points they are due. Remove the Project card and dice. 
	Note that when a project is completed, there is an additional effect that is applied immediately 
	(and which may cause further projects to be completed, in the same Phase).
	Projects that are completed may be “recycled” and placed in empty boxes until the total number of 
	completed Projects for the game is reached. 
	In either case the Country player may choose which Project is to be placed; 
	place the two dice showing their initial values of 0 (zero).

Victory Points and Victory Scale:
	Victory Points:
		Only the Jedburgh player gets Victory Points.
		The Victory Point value of a completed project is equal to its Completion Cost 
		(the two die values multiplied together at completion) 
		minus its Planned Cost (the two minimal values printed on the card multiplied together). 
		Record accumulated Victory Points on the track on the Game Mat. 
		Place a marker in the “+ 100” box when passing this total.

Example: 
	A Small Port is completed. The die values are 5 for Time and 4 for Budget, for a Completion Cost of 5 x 4 = 20. 
	The Planned Cost of the project is 4 x 3 = 12. 
	The Jedburgh player adds 8 Victory Points and the Country player adds 1 to either die value of any Project in play. 
	They choose Administrative System; adding 1 to the Budget value completes it on time and on budget, 
	so the Project is immediately scored. 
	Jedburgh does not get any Victory Points and the Country player moves the HN Influence Track marker 2 boxes 
	to the left.

Victory Scale. 
Add the total Planned Costs of all Projects that were completed. 
Compare this total to the number of Victory Points. 
Country wins if Blue’s Victory Points are less than 2.0 times this total. 
Jedburgh wins a Tactical Victory if they have 2.0 to 2.99 times the total, 
and a Strategic Victory if they have 3.0 times the total or more. 
We will likely have to vary these ratios after some playtesting.

Action cards when placed in a Network may be used for their value up to twice – first it is revealed, 
and then discarded when used for the second time. 

However, the assumption in the Turn End Phase is that work on a project proceeds unless its budget is zero 
(indicating it has not really been started yet). 
Meanwhile, project budgets can only ever be increased; any money already spent is a sunk cost 
(so think of the Highway System Project, which actually loses Victory points for the Jedburgh player when completed, 
as a stand-in for any number of make-work projects and initiatives that simply inject money into the 
local economy and would be viewed positively by the locals and their government).

Sequence of Play:
	Draw Cards Phase:
		Draw 1 card and add to hand. 
		Hand may be any size. When draw pile runs out, 
		shuffle discard pile and it becomes new draw pile. 
	Operations Phase: 
		Player with HN Influence plays first (if none, Jedburgh first). May play a Function Card 
		(choose 1 function), use an Action Card from their 
		Network, or pass. A player may always pass; a player 
		with no cards in their hand and no Action Cards in 
		their Network must pass. Phase ends when both 
		players pass in succession.
	Projects Phase:
		Projects that have met or exceeded 
		time and budget values are now completed and 
		scored. Execute completion effect of card. Remove 
		completed Project cards; if more Projects remain to be
 		played, Country will choose and replace Project cards in 
		empty boxes.
 	HN Influence Phase:
		Raise (if on Jedburgh side) or lower (if on Country side) Blue’s Victory Point level by a 
		number equal to the HN Influence value.
 	Turn End Phase:
		If all Projects to be played in the 
		game have been completed, end game and determine 
		Victory Level. If not, +1 Time of all Projects with 1+ 
		Budget and start new turn
